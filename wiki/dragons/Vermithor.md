# Vermithor

Champion · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Legendary · Positive affinities: Spearmen, Shieldbearers

Vermithor is a hybrid Physical/Tactical attacker with adjacent-ally **Protect**,
self-mitigation, and targeted suppression of Physical and Fire attackers. At 6 Stars,
**Bronze Bulwark** materially raises both Command damage rates and Protect reliability.

**Noble Sacrifice** is investment-sensitive: its opening Tactical vulnerability falls
from +20% at Habit Level 1 to 0% at Level 5, while its enemy damage suppression improves.
**Fury's Poise** does not unlock until 8 Stars, and **Vengeful Fury** does not unlock
until 10 Stars.

Exact wording and all Habit upgrade tracks: [JSON](../../data/dragons/vermithor.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Bronze Fury:** Rounds 2, 4, 6, 8 deal Physical and Tactical Damage to 2 Enemies within adjacency. Odd-numbered rounds have a chance to Protect the adjacent other Ally with the fewest troops. At 6+ Stars, the damage rates, Protect chance, and Vermithor's Physical/Fire mitigation increase.
- **Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance:** At Level 16+ in Vanguard, Vermithor gains +15 Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct; the Right Flank Ally receives 8% less Damage.
- **2 Stars — Noble Sacrifice:** Opening Tactical vulnerability is exchanged for five-round Physical suppression prioritizing a Warrior and Fire suppression prioritizing a Hunter.
- **4 Stars — Nimble Resilience:** Grants all-combat self Damage Received reduction and Initiative.
- **6 Stars — Bronze Bulwark:** Upgrades Bronze Fury and defines Protect as splitting Command and Habit damage with Vermithor.
- **8 Stars — Fury's Poise:** Each enemy Warrior adds Physical mitigation and each enemy Hunter adds Fire mitigation until combat ends.
- **10 Stars — Vengeful Fury:** Once per combat below 50% Max Troop Capacity, hits the lowest-Instinct enemy with Physical Damage and the lowest-Intelligence enemy with Tactical Damage.
