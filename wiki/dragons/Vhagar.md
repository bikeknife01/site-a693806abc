# Vhagar

Warrior · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Legendary · Shieldbearers, Archers, and Siege affinity. Durable Physical attacker with broad Taunt, recovery support, Advantage/Weakened control, and Bulwark stacking.

**Command — Fiery Bonds:** can Taunt three enemies every round—at double chance against Burn targets—and deals adjacency Physical Damage on even rounds.

**Vanguard — Warrior's Resilience:** -8% Damage Received; the Left Flank ally gains +16% Tactical Damage Dealt.

Constraint: Bulwark caps at 5 stacks and triggers same-lane Physical Damage on the third stack. The 10-Star Habit is confirmed as **Skyward Titan**.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Fiery Bonds:** Each Round: 25% chance to Taunt 3 Enemies in any lane for 2 round(s). This chance is doubled against targets afflicted with Burn. Even-numbered Rounds: Deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy within adjacency (Damage Rate: +120%).
- **Vanguard — Warrior's Resilience:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, reduce Damage Received by -8%. Increase Tactical Damage Dealt by +16% of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Ancestral Shield:** Start of Round 1: Reduce your Physical and Tactical Damage Received by -20% for 3 rounds. Start of Round 4: Increase your Recovery Received by +25.5% until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Battle Leader:** Start of Combat: Increase Physical Damage Dealt (excluding Basic Attacks) by +21.25% of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Eclipse Cover:** Rounds 3-7: 21% chance to grant Advantage (+20%) to the Ally with the most troops and afflict Weakened (-20%) on the Enemy with the most troops. Each effect lasts for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Blazing Onslaught:** Start of Round 1: Increase Fire Damage Received of 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank) and Physical Damage Received (excluding Basic Attacks) of 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) by +30.6% for 3 round(s).
- **10 Stars — Skyward Titan:** Each Round: 30% chance to gain 1 stack of Bulwark (Max 5 Stacks) until the end of combat. Each stack of Bulwark increases your Strength by +5% and reduces your Physical and Tactical Damage Received by -2%. Gain 3rd Stack of Bulwark: Deal Physical Damage (Damage Rate: +100%) to 1 Enemy in the same lane.
