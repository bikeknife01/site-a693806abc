# Thunderstrike

Warrior Elder · Cavalry affinity. Same-lane Physical attacker with Bleed and Stagger control.

**Command — Tail Whip:** physical damage on odd rounds; at 6 Stars, an even-round attack can Bleed.

**Vanguard — Warrior's Zeal:** +16% Physical Damage Dealt; the Left Flank ally gains +20 Instinct and Initiative.

Exact data: [JSON](../../data/dragons/thunderstrike.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Tail Whip:** Odd-numbered Rounds: Deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +100%). At 6+ Stars: Even-numbered Rounds: Deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +70%). 35% chance to afflict Bleed for 2 round(s) (Damage Rate: +20%).
- **Vanguard — Warrior's Zeal:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, increase your Physical Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Battle Rush:** At the start of round 1, increase your Initiative by +35% and reduce Instinct by -21% (enhanced by Strength) of 2 Enemies within adjacency. Each effect lasts for 3 rounds.
- **4 Stars — Dragon's Might:** Start of Combat: Increase your Physical Damage (Active Commands and Habits) Dealt by +11.2% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Barbed Lash:** Tail Whip gains: Even-numbered Rounds: Deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +70%). Additionally, there is a 35% chance to afflict the target with Bleed for 2 round(s) (Damage Rate: +20%). Bleed deals Physical Damage to the target each round.
- **8 Stars — Armor Break:** Start of Combat: Increase the Physical Damage Received by +5.67% of 1 opposing Enemy until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Staggering Assault:** Each Round: 10% to afflict Stagger on 1 Enemy in the same lane for 1 round(s). The duration is doubled (2 rounds) if you have Advantage.
