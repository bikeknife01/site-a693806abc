# Seasmoke

Champion Juvenile · Positive affinities: Cavalry, Archers · Negative affinity: Siege

Seasmoke removes enemy Positive effects, then turns cleanses into stacking Recovery
suppression. It also offers adjacent Fire support and a Panic-powered Physical
damage payoff.

Exact text and upgrade tracks: [JSON](../../data/dragons/seasmoke.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Cleansing Wrath:** Each Round: Cleanse up to 3 Positive effects from enemies. Each instance has a separate 20% chance and selects 1 Enemy in any lane. Rounds 3, 6, 9: Deal Fire Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +190%). At 6+ Stars: Each Round: When you Cleanse an enemy, afflict the target with 1 stack of Infectious Wrath (Max 3 stacks) for 3 rounds. Rounds 3, 6, 9: Deal Physical Damage to 2 Enemies within adjacency (Damage Rate: +30%), doubled if the target is afflicted with Panic.
- **Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct by +15. Reduce Damage Received by -8% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Clever Maneuver:** Start of Combat: Increase Intelligence and Initiative of the Ally with the highest Intelligence until the end of combat. Both effects are enhanced by Instinct.
- **4 Stars — Wind's Favor:** Start of Combat: Increase Initiative (enhanced by Initiative) of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Infectious Wrath:** Cleansing Wrath gains Infectious Wrath. Each stack reduces the target's Recovery Received; its Physical Damage is doubled against Panic.
- **8 Stars — Cunning Ferocity:** Start of Combat: Increase Intelligence of 2 adjacent Allies and their Fire Damage Dealt until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Loyal Bond:** Each Round: chance to grant Advantage to 2 other Allies above 50% troop capacity or Resistance to 2 other Allies below 50% troop capacity for 2 rounds.
