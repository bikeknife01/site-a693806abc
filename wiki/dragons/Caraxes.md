# Caraxes

Hunter · Age label: Juvenile · Rarity tier: Legendary · Positive affinities: Spearmen, Cavalry

## Strategic summary

Caraxes is an offensive Fire-damage Hunter with First-Strike, Slow, Burn, and low-troop enemy payoffs. Derived recommendation: deploy in Vanguard to amplify its Fire Damage and enhance a high-Strength Right Flank ally; seek a dependable First-Strike source for its command burst.

## Command — Infernal Burst

Rounds 3, 6, and 9: +100% Fire Damage to three enemies in any lane. With First-Strike this becomes +150%.

## Vanguard — Hunter's Wrath

At level 16+ in Vanguard: +16% self Fire Damage Dealt; Right Flank ally gets +20 Strength and +20 Initiative.
