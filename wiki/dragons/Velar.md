# Velar

Sentinel Elder · Shieldbearer affinity. Tactical team-support dragon with Advantage, Recovery, Cleanse, First-Strike, and Slow.

**Command — Whirlwind:** grants ally Advantage, deals three-target Tactical Damage, and at 10 Stars adds teamwide Bleed/Panic/Burn cleanse plus Recovery.

**Vanguard — Sentinel's Wit:** +16% Tactical Damage Dealt; the Left Flank ally gains +20 Instinct and Initiative.

Exact data: [JSON](../../data/dragons/velar.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Whirlwind:** Rounds 2, 4, 6, 8: 20% chance to grant 2 other Allies in any lane Advantage (+15%) for 2 round(s). Rounds 3, 5, 7, 9: Deal Tactical Damage to 3 Enemies in any lane (Damage Rate: +45%). At 10 Stars: Each Round: 12% chance to Cleanse 1 instance of Bleed, Panic, or Burn from each Ally. Rounds 2, 4, 6, 8: Apply Recovery to 3 Allies in any lane (Recovery Rate: +18%, enhanced by Initiative).
- **Vanguard — Sentinel's Wit:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, increase your Tactical Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Strategic Leader:** Start of Combat: Increase Tactical Damage Dealt by +17% of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Vanguard) until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Quick Reflexes:** Start of Combat: Increase your Instinct and Initiative by +22.4% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Gales of Power:** Rounds 2, 4, 6, 8: 14% chance to grant First-Strike to 3 Allies in any lane and afflict Slow on 3 Enemies in any lane. Each effect is checked separately per target and lasts for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Fierce Unity:** Start of Combat: Increase Strength and Instinct by +5% (enhanced by Initiative) of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Breath of Renewal:** Whirlwind gains: Each Round: 12% chance to Cleanse 1 instance of Bleed, Panic, or Burn from each Ally. This chance is checked separately per target. Rounds 2, 4, 6, 8: Apply Recovery to 3 Allies in any lane (Recovery Rate: +18%, enhanced by Initiative).
