# Vesper

Sentinel Elder · Shieldbearer affinity; Siege weakness. Tactical support dragon with Slow, Instinct scaling, mitigation, Resistance, and late-round Confusion.

**Command — Eventide Strike:** may Slow a same-lane enemy each round and strikes that lane with Tactical Damage on rounds 1, 3, 6, and 8.

**Vanguard — Sentinel's Wit:** +16% Tactical Damage Dealt; the Left Flank ally gains +20 Instinct and Initiative.

Exact data: [JSON](../../data/dragons/vesper.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Eventide Strike:** Each Round: 20% chance to afflict Slow on 1 Enemy in the same lane for 2 round(s). Rounds 1, 3, 6, 8: Deal Tactical Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +70%).
- **Vanguard — Sentinel's Wit:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, increase Tactical Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Strategic Leader:** Start of Combat: Increase Tactical Damage Dealt by +8% of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Vanguard) until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Dragon's Insight:** Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received by -4% and increase your Instinct by +5% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Savior's Waltz:** Each Round: 13% chance to apply Resistance (-10%) to yourself and 1 other Ally within adjacency for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Insightful Allies:** Start of Combat: Increase Instinct by +8% (enhanced by Instinct) of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Midnight Onslaught:** Rounds 6-10: 24% chance to afflict Confusion on 1 Enemy within adjacency for 2 round(s).
