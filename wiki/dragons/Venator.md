# Venator

Warrior Adult · Spearmen and Shieldbearer affinity. Physical Basic-Attack attacker that counters Hunters and gains late-round Double-Strike.

**Command — Feral Strike:** adds two separately targeted Physical hits after each Basic Attack; at 6 Stars, Basic Attacks also hit the Enemy with the fewest troops.

**Vanguard — Warrior's Zeal:** +16% Physical Damage Dealt; the Left Flank ally gains +20 Instinct and Initiative.

Exact data: [JSON](../../data/dragons/venator.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Feral Strike:** After each Basic Attack: deal 2 instances of Physical Damage to 1 Enemy in any lane (Damage Rate: +20%). Each damage instance selects its target separately. Rounds 4, 6, 8: 30% chance to gain Double-Strike for 2 round(s). At 6 Stars: Basic Attacks deal Physical Damage to the Enemy with the least troops (Damage Rate: +40%); the Double-Strike chance becomes 50%.
- **Vanguard — Warrior's Zeal:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, increase Physical Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Hunter's Bane:** Start of Combat: Reduce Intelligence by -36% (enhanced by Strength) of 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes Hunters) until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Dragon's Might:** Start of Combat: Increase your Physical Damage Dealt (excluding Basic Attacks) by +25% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Feral Precision:** Feral Strike gains: After each Basic Attack: Deal Physical Damage to the enemy with the least troops (Damage Rate: +40%). Rounds 4, 6, 8: The chance to gain Double-Strike is increased to 50%.
- **8 Stars — Armor Break:** Start of Combat: Increase the Physical Damage Received by +8% of 1 opposing Enemy until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Desperate Ambush:** Each Round: If you are below 50% troop capacity, deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes Hunters) (Damage Rate: +60%). Additionally, there is a 12% chance to afflict the target with Overwhelm for 2 round(s).
