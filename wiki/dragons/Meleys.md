# Meleys

Warrior · Rarity tier: Legendary · Cavalry and Archer affinity · Siege negative affinity. Physical attacker that pressures the fastest enemy with Laceration and can turn Basic Attacks back through Reflect.

Evidence class: **cross-site verified**. Dragonfire Hub and Dragonfire Sim publish matching mechanics and upgrade tracks, and the installed client corroborates names, targeting, affinities, Laceration, and Reflect. No current screenshot record was available on 2026-10-12.

**Command — Scarlet Strike:** on odd rounds, deals +135% Physical Damage to the enemy with the highest Initiative and applies Laceration. Laceration stacks up to 8 and each stack reduces Initiative by 7%, enhanced by Strength.

**Vanguard — Warrior's Resilience:** reduces own Damage Received by 8%; the Left Flank ally gains 16% Tactical Damage Dealt.

Exact data and all five Habit upgrade tracks: [JSON](../../data/dragons/meleys.json).

## Recommendation-critical coverage

- **8 Stars — Brisk Might:** Physical Damage Dealt scales with Initiative lead over the enemy target.
