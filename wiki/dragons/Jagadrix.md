# Jagadrix

Hunter · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinity: Spearmen

Jagadrix is a Vanguard-oriented Fire attacker that weakens enemy Instinct and
Initiative in its lane. At 10 Stars, **Cunning Whispers** gains a combat-long
highest-Instinct debuff and a multi-target Fire payoff against Tactical-damage
enemies, doubled when they are Panicked.

**Hunter's Wrath** increases Jagadrix's Fire Damage Dealt by 16% in Vanguard and
gives the Right Flank ally +20 Strength and Initiative. A Panic source and a
high-Strength Right Flank make the most of its kit.

Habits add Tactical-damage suppression, a late recovery/damage sequence,
Weakened, self Intelligence/Initiative, and the 10-Star command upgrade.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Cunning Whispers:** Each Round: 30% chance to reduce Instinct and Initiative by -15% (enhanced by Initiative) of 1 Enemy in the same lane for 2 round(s). Rounds 2, 5, 8: Deal Fire Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +120%). At 10 Stars: Round 1: reduce Instinct and Initiative by -6% (enhanced by Initiative) of the Enemy with the highest Instinct until the end of combat. Rounds 3, 6, 9: Deal Fire Damage to all Enemies that deal Tactical Damage (Damage Rate: +30%), doubled against targets afflicted with Panic.
- **Vanguard — Hunter's Wrath:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Fire Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Strength and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Enervate:** Start of Combat: Reduce Tactical Damage Dealt by -8% of 1 Enemy that deals Tactical Damage until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Second Wind:** Round 6: Increase your Damage Dealt by +10% until the end of combat. Recover your troops (Recovery Rate: +150%), then Nullify Recovery until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Whispering Sabotage:** Each Round: 25% chance to afflict Weakened (-10%) on 1 Enemy in the same lane for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Quick-Witted:** Start of Combat: Increase your Intelligence and Initiative by +12.5% until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Echoes of Deceit:** Cunning Whispers gains: Round 1: reduce Instinct and Initiative by -6% (enhanced by Initiative) of the Enemy with the highest Instinct until the end of combat. Rounds 3, 6, 9: Deal Fire Damage to all Enemies that deal Tactical Damage (Damage Rate: +30%), doubled against targets afflicted with Panic.
