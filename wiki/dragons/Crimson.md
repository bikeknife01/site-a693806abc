# Crimson

Hunter Adult · Positive affinities: Spearmen, Archers, Siege

Crimson is a Fire attacker and Stun/Weakened controller. In Vanguard, it gains Recovery Received and Intelligence while strengthening the Right Flank's Physical Damage. Taunt enables its stronger Weakened chance.

**Bloodscale Terror** Stuns on odd rounds, deals Fire Damage on rounds 2, 5, and 8, and gains stronger Stun plus stat reduction at 10 Stars. Habits add Tactical mitigation, self Intelligence, Weakened, troop-capacity thresholds, and the 10-Star upgrade.

Exact text and level tracks: [JSON](../../data/dragons/crimson.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Bloodscale Terror:** Odd-numbered Rounds: 20% chance to afflict Stun on 1 Enemy in any lane for 2 round(s). Rounds 2, 5, 8: Deal Fire Damage to 1 Enemy in any lane (Damage Rate: +140%). At 10 Stars: Round 1: The chance to Stun is increased to 40%. Even-numbered Rounds: 50% chance to reduce Instinct and Initiative by -12% (enhanced by Intelligence) of 1 Enemy in any lane for 2 round(s).
- **Vanguard — Hunter's Cunning:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Recovery Received by +20% and Intelligence by +25. Increase Physical Damage Dealt by +10% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Enervate:** At the start of combat, reduce Tactical Damage Dealt by -13% of 1 Enemy that deals Tactical Damage until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Dragon's Intellect:** Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received by -6% and increase your Intelligence by +12% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Bloodscale Fury:** Each Round: 18% chance to afflict Weakened (-20%) on 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritize targets NOT afflicted with Stun) for 2 round(s). This chance is doubled (35%) against targets afflicted with Taunt.
- **8 Stars — Unlikely Hero:** Start of Each Round: Increase Physical Damage Received (excluding Basic Attacks) and Fire Damage Received by +10% for all Enemies above 75% max troop capacity until the end of the round. Start of Each Round: Reduce Recovery Received by -20% for all Enemies below 25% max troop capacity until the end of the round.
- **10 Stars — Vermin's Bane:** Bloodscale Terror gains: Even-numbered Rounds: 50% chance to reduce Instinct and Initiative by -12% (enhanced by Intelligence) of the enemy with the highest Instinct for 2 round(s). Round 1: The chance to Stun is increased to 40%.
