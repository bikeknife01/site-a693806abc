# Shadowsong

Hunter · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Epic · Positive affinity: Cavalry · No displayed negative affinity

Shadowsong is a Fire attacker with Panic payoffs, Burn, Vulnerable, and opening stat/damage-received debuffs. **Breath of Fire** attacks two adjacent enemies on rounds 2, 5, and 8; Panic raises its damage, while the 10-Star upgrade adds two separately targeted Fire/Burn attempts.

**Vanguard — Hunter's Wrath:** at level 16+, +16% self Fire Damage Dealt and +20 Strength and Initiative for the Right Flank ally. Derived formation guidance: favor Vanguard only when that right-flank support and the self Fire bonus outweigh another dragon's Vanguard effect; pair with a reliable Panic source. Cavalry is the source-supported troop choice.

Recommendation-critical Habits include a round-1 adjacent Instinct/Initiative reduction, lane-prioritized Fire and non-Basic Physical Damage Received increases, recurring adjacent Vulnerable that doubles its chance against Panic, and permanent self Damage Received/Intelligence changes.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Breath of Fire:** Rounds 2, 5, 8: Deal Fire Damage to 2 Enemies within adjacency (Damage Rate: +100%), increased to +150% against Panic. At 10 Stars: deal Fire Damage to one Enemy in any lane (+60%) with 40% Burn chance, then a different Enemy (+30%) with 20% Burn chance.
- **Vanguard — Hunter's Wrath:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Fire Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Strength and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Ensnare:** Round 1: Reduce Instinct and Initiative by -18% (enhanced by Intelligence) of 2 Enemies within adjacency for 3 round(s).
- **4 Stars — Blazing Onslaught:** Start of Round 1: Increase Fire Damage Received of 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank) and Physical Damage Received (excluding Basic Attacks) of 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) by +15% for 3 round(s).
- **6 Stars — Scorched Earth:** Each Round: 10% chance to afflict Vulnerable (+15%) on 2 Enemies within adjacency for 2 round(s). This chance is doubled (20%) against targets afflicted with Panic.
- **8 Stars — Dragon's Intellect:** Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received by -5% and increase your Intelligence by +8.5% until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Blazing Conductor:** Breath of Fire gains: Rounds 2, 5, 8: Deal Fire Damage to 1 Enemy in any lane (Damage Rate: +60%) and there is a 40% chance to afflict the target with Burn for 2 round(s). Then deal Fire Damage to a different Enemy in any lane (Damage Rate: +30%) and there is a 20% chance to afflict the target with Burn for 2 round(s).
