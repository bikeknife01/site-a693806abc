# Rhysarion

Champion · Age label: Adult · Rarity tier: Epic · Positive affinities: Spearmen, Shieldbearers, Siege

Rhysarion is a hybrid attacker/recovery support. **Dawnsong** rewards Control
effects with stronger Fire Damage and gains two-target Recovery at 6 Stars.

Exact text and upgrade tracks: [JSON](../../data/dragons/rhysarion.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Dawnsong:** Rounds 1, 4, 7: Deal Physical Damage to 2 Enemies within adjacency (Damage Rate: +70%). Rounds 2, 5, 8: Deal Fire Damage to 3 Enemies in any lane (Damage Rate: +20%). This damage is increased by 1.5x if the target is afflicted with a Control effect (Damage Rate: +30%). At 6+ Stars: Rounds 2, 5, 8: Apply Recovery to 2 other Allies in any lane (Recovery Rate: +120%, enhanced by Intelligence).
- **Vanguard — Champion's Vigor:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Recovery Dealt by +15% and Initiative by +25. Increase the Damage Dealt by +8% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Ebbing Fury:** Start of Round 1: Reduce Damage Dealt of all Enemies AND Allies for 3 rounds. Start of Round 4: Apply Recovery to 3 Allies in any lane.
- **4 Stars — Sharp Resolve:** Start of Combat: Increase your Strength and Intelligence until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Echoing Melody:** Dawnsong gains: Rounds 2, 5, 8: Apply Recovery to 2 other Allies in any lane, enhanced by Intelligence.
- **8 Stars — Unbroken Devotion:** Start of Combat Increase Recovery Received of 2 other Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Inspiring Melody:** Each Round: chance to increase Initiative and grant Resistance to 1 other Ally within adjacency for 3 rounds.
