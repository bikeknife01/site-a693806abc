# Solstryker

Champion · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinity: Archers · No displayed negative affinity

Solstryker alternates Basic-Attack-triggered damage: odd rounds add Physical Damage to the Basic Attack target and may reduce its Physical Damage Dealt; even rounds deal Tactical Damage to three enemies, doubled against Vulnerable targets. **Oppressive Onslaught** adds recurring Overwhelm.

**Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance:** at level 16+, +15 Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct; the Right Flank ally gets -8% Damage Received. Archers are the source-supported troop choice.

Formation constraints and synergies: supply Vulnerable to unlock the even-round payoff. Steady Erosion applies one Strength-reduction stack per round to all enemies, capped at 10; Energy Drain and Amplified Drain reduce adjacent enemies' Strength and Initiative on rounds 1 and 4. Robust Insight raises self Strength and Instinct.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Tactical Onslaught:** Odd-numbered Rounds: After each Basic Attack, deal Physical Damage to target (+30%) and 20% chance reduce its Physical Damage Dealt by -12% for 2 rounds. Even-numbered Rounds: After each Basic Attack, deal Tactical Damage to 3 Enemies in any lane (+12.5%), doubled against Vulnerable.
- **Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct by +15. Reduce Damage Received by -8% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Steady Erosion:** At the start of each round, add 1 Stack of Steady Erosion (Max 10 Stacks) to all enemies. Each Stack of Steady Erosion reduces the target's Strength by -2% (enhanced by Strength) until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Energy Drain:** Round 1: Reduce Strength and Initiative by -15% of 2 Enemies within adjacency (enhanced by Instinct) for 3 round(s).
- **6 Stars — Oppressive Onslaught:** Each Round: 10% chance to afflict Overwhelm on 1 Enemy in any lane for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Robust Insight:** Start of Combat: Increase your Strength and Instinct by +12.5% until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Amplified Drain:** Round 4: Reduce Strength and Initiative by -15% of 2 Enemies within adjacency (enhanced by Instinct) for 5 round(s).
