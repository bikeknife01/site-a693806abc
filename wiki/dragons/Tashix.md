# Tashix

Hunter · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Epic · Archers affinity; Siege penalty. Stacking Fire attacker with broad tactical and initiative debuffs.

**Command — Shimmering Mirage:** each round can gain Mirage (up to 10 stacks); rounds 3, 6, and 9 deal +200% Fire Damage. Each Mirage stack grants +2.5% Fire Damage Dealt.

**Vanguard — Hunter's Cunning:** +20% Recovery Received and +25 Intelligence; grants the Right Flank ally +10% Physical Damage Dealt.

Habits: Enervate (-10.5% to -21% Tactical Damage Dealt), Dragon’s Cunning (+12–24% Intelligence and -10–20% Instinct), Cunning Ruse (10–20% Weakened chance), Battle Guile (-5% to -10.5% Instinct/Initiative), Veiled Ambush (150–500% Fire Damage).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Shimmering Mirage:** Each Round: 50% chance to gain 1 stack of Mirage (Max 10 Stacks). Rounds 3, 6, 9: Deal Fire Damage to 1 Enemy within adjacency (Damage Rate: +200%). Mirage: Increase your Fire Damage Dealt by +2.5% per stack. Max 10 stacks.
- **Vanguard — Hunter's Cunning:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, increase your Recovery Received by +20% and Intelligence by +25. Increase Physical Damage Dealt by +10% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Enervate:** Start of Combat: Reduce Tactical Damage Dealt by -10% of 1 Enemy that deals Tactical Damage until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Dragon's Cunning:** Start of Combat: Increase your Intelligence by +12% and reduce Instinct by -10% (enhanced by Initiative) of 2 Enemies within adjacency. Each effect lasts until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Cunning Ruse:** Each Round: 25% chance to gain 1 stack of Mirage (Max 10 Stacks). Each stack of Mirage increases your Fire Damage Dealt by +2.5% until the end of combat. Each Round: If you have 4 or more stacks of Mirage, there is a 10% chance to afflict 3 Enemies in any lane with Weakened (-15%) for 2 round(s). This chance is checked separately for each target and is doubled (20%) against targets that deal Tactical Damage.
- **8 Stars — Battle Guile:** Start of Combat: Reduce Instinct and Initiative by -5% (enhanced by Initiative) of 3 Enemies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Veiled Ambush:** Each Round: 25% chance to gain 1 stack of Mirage (Max 10 Stacks). Each stack of Mirage increases your Fire Damage Dealt by +2.5% until the end of combat. Each Round: If you have 7 or more stacks of Mirage, deal Fire Damage (Damage Rate: +150%) to the Enemy with the most troops. This effect can only trigger once per combat.
