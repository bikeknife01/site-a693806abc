# Shadowrend

Warrior · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinities: Shieldbearers, Siege

Shadowrend is a Vanguard Physical attacker with recurring Panic and a Round-9
hybrid burst. Its late-round habits provide allied stats, Advantage, and Physical/
Tactical damage to complement Midnight Aura.

Exact text and upgrade tracks: [JSON](../../data/dragons/shadowrend.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Eclipse Fervor:** Each Round: 25% chance to afflict Panic (Damage Rate: +20%) on 1 Enemy within adjacency for 2 rounds. Rounds 4, 7, 9, 10: Deal Physical Damage to 2 Enemies within adjacency (Damage Rate: +80%). At 10 Stars: On Round 9: Deal Physical and Tactical Damage to 3 Enemies in any lane (Damage Rate: +100% per type).
- **Vanguard — Warrior's Zeal:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Physical Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Midnight Aura:** Start of Rounds 7-10: Increase Strength and Instinct (enhanced by Initiative) of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of the round.
- **4 Stars — Nimble Resilience:** Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received and increase your Initiative until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Fueled by Darkness:** Each Round: chance to grant Advantage to 2 Allies in any lane for 2 rounds, doubled while Midnight Aura is active.
- **8 Stars — Midnight Mastery:** Start of Rounds 7-10: Increase Physical and Tactical Damage Dealt of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of the round.
- **10 Stars — Event Horizon:** Eclipse Fervor gains: On Round 9: Deal Physical and Tactical Damage to 3 Enemies in any lane (Damage Rate: +100% per type).
