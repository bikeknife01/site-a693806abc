# Arrax

Warrior · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinities: Shieldbearers, Archers

## Strategic summary

Arrax is a Physical-damage Warrior with defensive support and a Bleed payoff. Derived recommendation: use Arrax in the Vanguard to reduce its own Damage Received and give the Left Flank +16% Tactical Damage Dealt. Pair with a Bleed source to raise **Sudden Strike**'s Weakened chance to 50%.

## Command — Sudden Strike

- Rounds 2, 4, 6, and 8: 25% chance to apply Weakened to one adjacent enemy; doubled against Bleeding targets.
- Rounds 2, 4, 5, 6, and 8: +40% Physical Damage to two adjacent enemies.

## Vanguard — Warrior's Resilience

At level 16+ in Vanguard: -8% self Damage Received and +16% Tactical Damage Dealt for the Left Flank ally.
