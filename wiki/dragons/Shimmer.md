# Shimmer

Sentinel · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinities: Cavalry and Siege · No displayed negative affinity

Shimmer is Tactical/Recovery support. **Unbreakable Loyalty** buffs the other ally with the highest Strength, attacks two adjacent enemies on rounds 2, 4, 7, and 9, and at 6+ Stars recovers two other allies in any lane; Recovery doubles for targets with Resistance.

**Vanguard — Sentinel's Presence:** at level 16+, +15% self Recovery Dealt and +25 Instinct; the Left Flank ally gains +16% Fire Damage Dealt. Derived formation guidance: pair Shimmer with a Resistance source and a Fire-focused Left Flank when using this Vanguard effect. Cavalry or Siege are source-supported troop choices.

Habits add lane-prioritized non-Basic Physical and Tactical ally damage, self mitigation/Instinct, Recovery Received for two allies, and a recurring chance to give the highest-Strength other ally Physical Damage and First-Strike.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Unbreakable Loyalty:** Each Round: 30% chance to increase Strength (+18%, enhanced by Instinct) and Initiative (+9%, enhanced by Instinct) of the other Ally with highest Strength for 2 rounds. Rounds 2, 4, 7, 9: Deal Tactical Damage to 2 Enemies within adjacency (+50%). At 6+ Stars apply Recovery to 2 other Allies in any lane (+30%, doubled with Resistance).
- **Vanguard — Sentinel's Presence:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Recovery Dealt by +15% and Instinct by +25. Increase Fire Damage Dealt by +16% of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Crushing Force:** Start of Round 1: Increase the Physical Damage Dealt (excluding Basic Attacks) of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank) and the Tactical Damage Dealt of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) by +9% for 3 round(s).
- **4 Stars — Dragon's Insight:** Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received by -4% and increase your Instinct by +5% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Loyal Shield:** Unbreakable Loyalty gains: Rounds 2, 4, 7, 9: Apply Recovery to 2 other Allies in any lane (Recovery Rate: +30%, enhanced by Instinct). The amount of Recovery is doubled if the target has Resistance (Recovery Rate: +60%, enhanced by Instinct).
- **8 Stars — Unbroken Devotion:** Start of Combat: Increase Recovery Received by +15% of 2 other Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Sneak Attack:** Each Round: 14% chance to increase Physical Damage Dealt by +10% and grant First-Strike to the Other Ally with the highest Strength for 2 round(s).
