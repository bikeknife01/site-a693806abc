# Daemoros

Warrior Adult · Positive affinity: Archers

Daemoros is an adjacent Physical attacker with Burn, Panic, and Confusion control. Derived recommendation: Vanguard placement rewards a Tactical-damage Left Flank with Instinct and Initiative.

**Shadowflame** hits an adjacent enemy on odd rounds and can apply Burn. Habits add Panic with lane priorities, self Strength/Initiative, adjacent Confusion, and adaptive self damage resistance.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Shadowflame:** Odd-numbered Rounds: Deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy within adjacency (Damage Rate: +125%). Additionally, there is a 20% chance to afflict the target with Burn (Damage Rate: +20%) for 2 round(s).
- **Vanguard — Warrior's Zeal:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Physical Damage (Active Commands and Habits) Dealt by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Instill Fear:** Each Round: 50% chance to reduce Intelligence and Instinct by -25% (enhanced by Strength) and afflict Panic (Damage Rate: +20%) on 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank). Each effect lasts for 2 round(s).
- **4 Stars — Powerful Reflexes:** Start of Combat: Increase your Strength and Initiative by +27.2% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Shroud of Shadows:** Odd-numbered Rounds: 30% chance to afflict Confusion on 1 Enemy within adjacency for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Darkening Fear:** Each Round: 25% chance to reduce Intelligence and Instinct by -25% (enhanced by Strength) and afflict Panic (Damage Rate: +20%) on 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank). Each effect lasts for 2 round(s).
- **10 Stars — Phantom's Veil:** Start of Each Round Reduce either your Physical, Tactical, OR Fire Damage Received by -15% until the end of the round.
