# Dawnseeker

Sentinel Elder · Positive affinity: Spearmen · Negative affinity: Siege

Dawnseeker is a Tactical/Recovery support dragon and a valuable First-Strike provider. It is likely strongest in Vanguard, where it improves its Recovery and supports a Fire-damage Left Flank.

**Radiant Wings** provides self Instinct/Initiative, same-lane Tactical Damage, adjacent Recovery, and a six-star rate upgrade. Habits boost Tactical and Fire allies, Recovery Received, Initiative, and First-Strike.

The 10-Star Habit is **First Light**, which grants First-Strike while increasing allied Intelligence and Instinct.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Radiant Wings:** Each Round: 30% chance to increase your Instinct and Initiative by +20% for 1 round(s). Rounds 1, 2, 4, 7: Deal Tactical Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +50%). Rounds 2, 5, 8: Apply Recovery to 2 Allies within adjacency (Rate: +30%, enhanced by Initiative). At 6+ Stars Rounds 1, 2: Increase the Tactical Damage Rate to +100%, and increase the Recovery Rate to +60%.
- **Vanguard — Sentinel's Presence:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Recovery Dealt by +15% and Instinct by +25. Increase Fire Damage Dealt by +16% of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Tactical Inferno:** Start of Round 1: Increase the Tactical Damage Dealt of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank) and the Fire Damage Dealt of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) by +9% for 3 round(s).
- **4 Stars — Unbroken Devotion:** Start of Combat: Increase Recovery Received by +15% of 2 other Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Sunbreak:** Radiant Wings gains: Rounds 1, 2: Increase the Tactical Damage Rate to +100%, and increase the Recovery Rate to +60%.
- **8 Stars — Wind's Favor:** Start of Combat: Increase Initiative by +8% (enhanced by Initiative) of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — First Light:** Start of Round 1: Increase Intelligence and Instinct (enhanced by Initiative) by +5% of 3 Allies in any lane for 3 round(s). Start of Rounds 1-3: 20% chance to grant First-Strike to 2 other Allies in any lane until the end of the round.
