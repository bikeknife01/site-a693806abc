# Feskar

Champion Elder · Positive affinity: Cavalry · Negative affinity: Siege

Feskar suppresses high-Strength Physical attackers, targets low-troop enemies, and becomes a Burn payoff at six stars. Vanguard placement increases all three core stats and protects the Right Flank.

**Calculated Assault** weakens Physical-damage dealers and strikes the enemy with the fewest troops; its six-star Fire clause is stronger against Burn. Habits provide Physical resistance, Instinct support, self Intelligence/Initiative, and Stagger against Warriors.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Calculated Assault:** Each Round: 20% chance to reduce the Physical Damage Dealt (excluding Basic Attacks) by -12% of the Enemy with the highest Strength for 2 rounds(s). Rounds 2, 4, 7, 9: Deal Tactical Damage to the Enemy with the least troops (Damage Rate: +100%). At 6+ Stars: Rounds 3, 5, 8, 10: Deal Fire Damage to all Enemies that deal Physical Damage (excluding Basic Attacks) (Damage Rate: +40%). This damage is increased by 1.5x against targets afflicted with Burn (Damage Rate: +60%).
- **Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct by +15. Reduce Damage Received by -8% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Resilient Bond:** Start of Combat: Grant yourself and 1 other Ally within adjacency 1 stack of Resilient Bond. At the start of each round, if the target Ally retreated in the previous round, grant yourself 1 stack of Resilient Bond. Each stack of Resilient Bond reduces the target's Physical Damage Received (excluding Basic Attacks) by -6% until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Insightful Allies:** Start of Combat: Increase Instinct by +10% (enhanced by Instinct) of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Emerald Inferno:** Calculated Assault gains: Rounds 3, 5, 8, 10: Deal Fire Damage to all Enemies that deal Physical Damage (excluding Basic Attacks) (Damage Rate: +40%). This damage is increased by 1.5x against targets afflicted with Burn (Damage Rate: +60%).
- **8 Stars — Quick-Witted:** Start of Combat: Increase your Intelligence and Initiative by +16% until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Unyielding Grasp:** Each Round: 10% chance to Stagger 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes Warriors) for 3 round(s).
