# Arulix

Champion Elder · Positive affinity: Cavalry

## Strategic summary

Arulix is a broad counter-controller: it suppresses Fire and Physical-specialized enemies, inflicts Overwhelm or Stagger, and can copy relevant effects. Derived recommendation: use it in Vanguard to improve its three core stats and protect the Right Flank.

## Command — Gleaming Spiral

- Each round: chance to reduce Fire Damage Dealt of all Fire-damage enemies.
- Selected rounds: Tactical Damage to enemies that deal non-Basic Physical Damage.
- At 6+ Stars: recurring, scaling Physical Damage to two enemies.

## Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance

At level 16+ in Vanguard: +15 self Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct; Right Flank ally receives -8% Damage Received.
