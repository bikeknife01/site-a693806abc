# Nyrena

Champion · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinities: Shieldbearers, Siege

Nyrena weakens enemy Physical Damage, benefits from Burn, and combines Fire and
Tactical damage. Its late-game habits add Fire support, Tile Damage, and defensive
Physical mitigation—especially while defending.

Exact text and upgrade tracks: [JSON](../../data/dragons/nyrena.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Undermine:** Each Round: 10% chance to reduce Physical Damage Dealt of 3 Enemies in any lane by -10% for 1 round(s). The duration is doubled (2 rounds) against targets afflicted with Burn. Rounds 1, 3: Deal Fire Damage to 3 Enemies in any lane (Damage Rate: +20%). Rounds 5, 7, 9: Deal Tactical Damage to 1 Enemy in the same lane (Damage Rate: +80%).
- **Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct by +15. Reduce Damage Received by -8% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Battle Dread:** Start of Combat: Reduce the Strength and Initiative (enhanced by Intelligence) of 3 Enemies in any lane by -4% until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Mindful Synergy:** Start of Combat: Increase the Intelligence and Instinct (enhanced by Initiative) of 3 Allies in any lane by +4% until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Deepen The Breach:** Start of Round 1: Increase your Fire Damage Dealt for 5 rounds. Start of Round 6: Increase the Fire Damage Dealt of 1 other Ally within adjacency for 5 rounds. After Combat: Increase your Tile Damage Dealt to the tile's durability.
- **8 Stars — Dragon's Ire:** Start of Combat: Increase your Tactical and Fire Damage Dealt by +4% until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — The Long Siege:** Start of Rounds 6-10: Reduce the Physical Damage Received of 3 Allies in any lane by -5% until the end of the round. When Defending, double this effect (-10%).
