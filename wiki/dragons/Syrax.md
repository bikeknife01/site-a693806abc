# Syrax

Sentinel · Age label: Adult · Rarity tier: Legendary · Spearmen and Archers affinity; Siege penalty. Tactical/fire support with recovery, Resistance, and cleansing.

**Command — Blazing Fury:** buffs a Fire ally with Fire Damage Dealt and First-Strike, deals adjacent Tactical Damage, and at 6 Stars recovers the lowest-troop ally with a Slow payoff and possible Resistance.

**Vanguard — Sentinel's Wit:** +16% Tactical Damage; grants the Left Flank ally +20 Instinct and Initiative.

Habits: Mindful Synergy (6.5–13%), Flight Mastery (±6–12% Initiative), Strategic Revival (50–100% Recovery Rate and 25–50% Resistance chance), Tactical Inferno (18–36% damage boosts), Mother’s Mercy (14–35% cleanse chance).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Blazing Fury:** Each Round: 20% chance to increase Fire Damage Dealt by +10% and First-Strike to 1 Ally in any lane, prioritizing Fire Damage allies, for 2 round(s). Rounds 1, 4, 6, 9: Deal Tactical Damage to 1 Enemy within adjacency (Damage Rate: +110%). At 6+ Stars: Rounds 2, 5, 8: Recover the Ally with the fewest troops (Recovery Rate: +60%, enhanced by Initiative); increased to +90% if any Enemy is afflicted with Slow. 30% chance to grant Resistance (-20%) for 2 round(s).
- **Vanguard — Sentinel's Wit:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, increase your Tactical Damage Dealt by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Mindful Synergy:** Start of Combat: Increase the Intelligence and Instinct (enhanced by Initiative) of 3 Allies in any lane by +7.8% until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Flight Mastery:** Start of Combat: Increase Initiative by +7.2% (enhanced by Instinct) of 3 Allies in any lane and reduce Initiative by -7% (enhanced by Instinct) of 3 Enemies in any lane until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Strategic Revival:** Blazing Fury gains: Rounds 2, 5, 8: Apply Recovery to the Ally with the least troops (Recovery Rate: +60%, enhanced by Initiative). The amount of Recovery is increased by 1.5x (+90%) if an Enemy is afflicted with Slow. Additionally, there is a 30% chance to grant the target Resistance (-20%) for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Tactical Inferno:** Start of Round 1: Increase the Tactical Damage Dealt of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank) and the Fire Damage Dealt of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) by +18% for 3 round(s).
- **10 Stars — Mother's Mercy:** Each Round: 14% chance to Cleanse 2 Negative and 1 Control effects from 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes allies afflicted with a Control effect).
