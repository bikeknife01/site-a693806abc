# Vaeldra

Warrior Elder · Spearmen affinity. Taunt-focused frontline dragon with Physical Damage amplification and tactical support.

**Command — Lure:** can Taunt three enemies each round and deals adjacent two-target Physical Damage on odd rounds.

**Vanguard — Warrior's Resilience:** -8% self Damage Received; grants the Left Flank ally +16% Tactical Damage Dealt.

Exact data: [JSON](../../data/dragons/vaeldra.json).

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Lure:** Each Round: 25% chance to Taunt 3 Enemies in any lane for 2 round(s). Odd-numbered Rounds: Deal Physical Damage to 2 Enemies within adjacency (Damage Rate: +45%).
- **Vanguard — Warrior's Resilience:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard, reduce your Damage Received by -8%. Increase Tactical Damage Dealt by +16% of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — Dragon's Valor:** Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received by -6% and increase your Strength by +10.2% until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Ensnare:** Round 1: Reduce Instinct and Initiative by -18% (enhanced by Intelligence) of 2 Enemies within adjacency for 3 round(s).
- **6 Stars — Tempting Distraction:** When you afflict an Enemy with Taunt: Increase their Physical Damage Received (excluding Basic Attacks) and Fire Damage Received by +6% for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Infernal Force:** Start of Round 1: Increase the Fire Damage Dealt of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank) and the Physical Damage Dealt (excluding Basic Attacks) of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) by +14.4%. Each effect lasts for 3 rounds.
- **10 Stars — Siren's Call:** Start of Round 1: Reduce your Physical Damage Received by -10% for 3 round(s). Start of Rounds 1-3: 40% chance to Taunt all Enemies until the end of the round. Targets that are already afflicted with Taunt will be afflicted with Stagger instead.
