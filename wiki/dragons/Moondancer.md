# Moondancer

Warrior · Age label: Juvenile · Rarity tier: Legendary · Positive affinities: Shieldbearers, Archers · Negative affinity: Siege

Moondancer is a Sentinel-linked Physical attacker built around **Rising Tide**.
Its command lets another Sentinel generate stacks whenever it deals Tactical Damage
or Recovery; stacks reduce Moondancer's Damage Received and eventually amplify its
adjacent Physical attacks.

Build it with a Tactical-damage or recovery Sentinel and an Advantage source.
New Moon and Full Moon create stacks, while Blood Moon adds scaling Physical Damage
and Bleed. Eclipsing Strike applies a high-troop-target damage debuff and gains an
Initiative debuff when Rising Tide is sufficiently stacked.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Crescent Blade:** Round 1: Grant Crescent Blade to 1 other Sentinel Ally in any lane until the end of combat. When that Ally deals Tactical Damage or applies Recovery, there is a 50% chance to grant Moondancer 1 stack of Rising Tide (maximum 8; no more than once per round). Each Rising Tide stack reduces Moondancer's Damage Received by -2%. Even-numbered Rounds: Deal Physical Damage to 2 adjacent Enemies (Damage Rate: +75%). At 6+ Stars: increase the Damage Rate to +85%; double it to +170% with 4+ Rising Tide stacks.
- **Vanguard — Warrior's Zeal:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Physical Damage Dealt (Active Commands/Habits) by +16%. Increase Instinct and Initiative by +20 of the Ally deployed in the Left Flank.
- **2 Stars — New Moon:** Rounds 1, 3, 5: 25% chance to gain Rising Tide; double the chance if an Ally has Advantage. Odd-numbered Rounds: increase Instinct by +9% (enhanced by Initiative) and Tactical Damage Dealt by +6% of 1 other Sentinel Ally for 2 round(s). At 4+ Rising Tide stacks, these effects are increased by 1.5 times.
- **4 Stars — Reactive Instincts:** Start of Combat: Increase Instinct by +22% and Initiative by +11% of the Ally with the highest Instinct until the end of combat (enhanced by Strength).
- **6 Stars — Full Moon:** Rounds 6, 8, 10: 25% chance to gain Rising Tide, doubled if an Ally has Advantage; additionally gain 1 stack if you have the fewest troops. Crescent Blade gains Physical Damage (Damage Rate: +85%), doubled at 4+ Rising Tide stacks.
- **8 Stars — Blood Moon:** Each Round: with 4+ Rising Tide stacks, increase your Physical Damage Dealt by +12.5% until the end of the round. Odd-numbered Rounds: 25% chance to afflict Bleed on 2 adjacent Enemies for 2 round(s), doubled with 6+ Rising Tide stacks.
- **10 Stars — Eclipsing Strike:** Each Round: 20% chance to reduce Damage Dealt by -18% of the Enemy with the most troops for 2 round(s). With 6+ Rising Tide stacks, double the chance to 40% and reduce its Initiative by -25% (enhanced by Initiative).
