# Kalspire

Champion Adult · Positive affinities: Cavalry, Shieldbearers, Siege

Kalspire is a hybrid damage/control Vanguard. **Tactical Strike** follows Basic
Attacks with Tactical Damage and Bleed; its 6-Star upgrade adds adjacent Physical
Damage and Panic. Its Vanguard effect raises all three core stats and reduces the
Right Flank ally's Damage Received.

The Habit suite supplies self stats, enemy Strength/Intelligence reduction, the
command upgrade, durability, and a 10-Star opening defense plus five-round damage
suppression against the highest-Strength and highest-Intelligence enemies.

## Recommendation-critical source coverage

- **Command — Tactical Strike:** After each Basic Attack: Deal Tactical Damage to the target (Damage Rate: +50%). 30% chance to afflict Bleed on the target and 1 adjacent Enemy for 2 round(s). At 6+ Stars: Deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy adjacent to the target (excluding the original target) (Damage Rate: +30%). 18% chance to afflict Panic on the target and adjacent Enemy for 2 round(s).
- **Vanguard — Champion's Brilliance:** At Level 16+ and deployed in the Vanguard Increase your Strength, Intelligence, and Instinct by +15. Reduce Damage Received by -8% of the Ally deployed in the Right Flank.
- **2 Stars — Robust Insight:** Start of Combat: Increase your Strength and Instinct by +20 until the end of combat.
- **4 Stars — Battle Cunning:** Start of Combat: Reduce Strength and Intelligence by -6% (enhanced by Instinct) of 3 Enemies until the end of combat.
- **6 Stars — Tactical Assault:** Tactical Strike gains: after each Basic Attack, deal Physical Damage to 1 Enemy adjacent to the target (excluding the original target) (Damage Rate: +30%). 18% chance to afflict Panic on the target and an adjacent Enemy for 2 round(s).
- **8 Stars — Dragon's Insight:** Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received by -6% and increase your Instinct by +12% until the end of combat.
- **10 Stars — Radiant Conqueror:** Start of Round 1: Reduce your Damage Received by -50% for 1 round, then afflict Stun on yourself for 1 round. Start of Round 2: Reduce Physical Damage Dealt (excluding Basic Attacks) of the Enemy with the highest Strength and Fire Damage Dealt of the Enemy with the highest Intelligence by -10% for 5 round(s).
