# Antares

Hunter · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinity: Archers · Negative affinity: Siege

## Strategic summary

Antares is an offensive Fire-damage debuffer whose six-star command becomes a
multi-target payoff for enemies afflicted with Slow. Antares is most distinctive in
the Vanguard: **Hunter's Wrath** raises Antares's Fire Damage Dealt by 16% and gives
the Right Flank ally +20 Strength and +20 Initiative.

Recommended construction: place Antares in the Vanguard, supply Slow through an
ally, use Archers with Antares, and place a Physical-damage-oriented ally on the
Right Flank to exploit the flat Strength and Initiative bonuses.

## Command — Relentless Pursuit

- Each Round: 20% chance to afflict Vulnerable (+10%) on 1 Enemy within adjacency
  for 2 rounds.
- Rounds 3, 6, and 9: deals Fire Damage to 2 adjacent enemies at +65% Damage Rate.
- At 6+ Stars: each round, deals Fire Damage to 3 Slowed enemies in any lane at +20%
  Damage Rate before Habit upgrades.

## Vanguard — Hunter's Wrath

At level 16+ in the Vanguard, increases Antares's Fire Damage Dealt by 16% and
increases the Right Flank ally's Strength and Initiative by 20.
