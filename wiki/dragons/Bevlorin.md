# Bevlorin

Champion · Age label: Elder · Rarity tier: Rare · Positive affinity: Spearmen

## Strategic summary

Bevlorin combines anti-Fire disruption, multi-target damage, Recovery, and an ally damage boost. Derived recommendation: use it in Vanguard with a damage-focused Right Flank; its later Habits reinforce sustained Recovery and team stat support.

## Command — Nature's Reckoning

- Each round: chance to reduce three Fire-damage enemies' Fire Damage Dealt.
- Rounds 1, 5, 9: multi-target Physical Damage; rounds 3, 7: same-lane Fire Damage.
- At 6+ Stars: Recovery for three allies on odd-numbered rounds, enhanced by Strength.

## Vanguard — Champion's Vigor

At level 16+ in Vanguard: +15% self Recovery Dealt, +25 Initiative, and +8% Damage Dealt for the Right Flank ally.
