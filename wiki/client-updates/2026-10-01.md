## Strategist-relevant changes

### Commander trait affinities

The Commander table associates five production-ready Commanders with two dragon traits each:

- Corlys: Cunning, Relentless
- Criston: Dominant, Stalwart
- Daemon: Volatile, Ruthless
- Young Alicent: Willful, Cunning
- Young Rhaenyra: Nimble, Stalwart

Aegon, Aemond, and Rhaenys still did not have trait affinities in this table. All eight Commander rows also gained a base-power progression link and an event/category link.

### Commander talent kits

Criston Cole, Daemon Targaryen, and young Alicent no longer pointed at placeholder dragon habits. Their three talent slots gained dedicated abilities, effects, progression, localization, and icons.

- Criston: The Kingmaker; Vanguard's Challenge; Strike and Vanish
- Daemon: The Rogue Prince; Cloaked in Gold; No Quarter
- Young Alicent: The Seven's Grace; Steel Beneath Silk; Hightower's Beacon

The installed mechanics include Command/Talent damage Reflect for a Vanguard, lane-specific mitigation and offense, player-attack bonuses, Fire amplification, Cleanse/Recovery/Immunity support, and propagation of Vanguard Control effects.

Corlys and young Rhaenyra received final talent icons. Their existing talent data also changed materially:

- The Sea Snake applies 3 Vulnerable stacks, or 5 with Relentless, and its trigger chance doubles with Relentless. Its bonus reduces Tactical Damage Dealt by one Tactical attacker when Relentless is present.
- Blockade mitigates Physical and Tactical damage, doubles with Cunning, and its bonus targets one Physical attacker when Cunning is present.
- Driftmark's Snare keeps Undertow but changes its rank bonus from reducing Tactical Damage Dealt to reducing Recovery Received.
- The Realm's Delight raises its non-player multiplier from 1.5x to 2x and separates Strength from post-combat Tile Damage wording.
- Swift Counsel accepts Nimble or Stalwart composition, gives 2 Resistance stacks to the lowest-troop ally or 4 with a matching trait, and adds trait-gated Tile Damage to its bonus.
- Heir's Reckoning replaces Vulnerable with Weakened, accepts Nimble or Stalwart composition, and adds trait-gated Tile Damage to its bonus.

### Dragon and combat semantics

- Meleys gained explicit Relentless and Physical DPS labels.
- Queen's Vengeance describes Reflect as a 100% chance against Basic Attacks for one round. Reflect is generalized as a chance to split specified damage and is limited to one Reflect (Damage) effect per target.
- A new `ability_label_reflect_damage` label supports that distinction.
- Smoketail's Ember Snare calls its ongoing Fire damage effect `Burn`.
- Four Alpha savage-dragon spawn rows were added: Champion, Hunter, Sentinel, and Warrior.
- Targeting and condition rows gained explicit Left Flank, Right Flank, Vanguard, Dominant/Stalwart, and Control-status variants. Several older generic flank and misspelled `haslable` rows were removed.

Seven dragon rows changed. Six only lost `Content:Dragon:*` tagging: Tairax, Moondancer, Sheepstealer, Sunfyre, Tessarion, and Vermax. Meleys was the only row with new combat labels.

## Other notable changes

- `DailyQuestTable` migrated from protobuf to JSON.
- Daily quest UI wording changed `Spend Stamina` to `Send Marches`.
- New dragon chest/event localization was staged, including featured-dragon pairings and Moondancer event content.
- Quest/task progression data changed heavily, but much of it appeared to be campaign staging and must not be treated as live balance without activation evidence.
- No existing logical `Westeros_*` map resource changed content.

## Evidence limits

The schema-less protobuf decoder exposes wiring and values but not authoritative semantic field names. Commander and combat behavior above is corroborated by both table wiring and installed localization. Client presence does not prove release, server activation, player availability, or final balance.
