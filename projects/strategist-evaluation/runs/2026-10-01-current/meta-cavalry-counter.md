I’ll reconstruct the Rank 6 enemy kit and evaluate the full canonical roster under the repository’s counter decision contract, without touching evaluation artifacts.

The matchup is a physical-pressure formation with Moondancer’s Sentinel-enabled Rising Tide engine and Vhagar’s lane-centered threat, while Shieldbearers create a clear Cavalry troop-counter opportunity. I’m narrowing the full roster by exact Rank 6 mechanics, then comparing direct suppression/protection against control-heavy disruption.

A pure Cavalry-affinity trio is attractive, but the strongest exact counter clauses are split: Vermithor directly suppresses a Warrior and can Protect against Command/Habit damage, while Malachite mitigates Velar’s Tactical pressure and sustains the formation. The final slot turns on whether enemy-buff removal or physical-dealer control disrupts more of the engine.

### Recommended Formation

**Best-supported counter:** **Malachite / Vermithor / Arulix**  
**Formation troop type:** **Cavalry**

### Why it works

- **Guaranteed opening suppression:** Vermithor reduces one enemy Warrior’s Physical Damage Dealt by **15% for Rounds 1–5**, prioritizing Warriors. Both Vhagar and Moondancer qualify, so the effect is certain but its target between them is not.
- **Formation-wide stat pressure:** Arulix reduces all enemies’ Strength and Intelligence by **8% for the combat** at Habit Level 5. The Strength reduction directly pressures Vhagar’s and Moondancer’s Strength-driven Physical output, although the unknown damage formula prevents quantifying the reduction.
- **Tactical defense and sustain:** Malachite has a **35% each-round chance** to grant both other allies **16% Tactical Damage Received reduction** for two rounds. That directly applies to Velar’s Tactical Command damage. Malachite also supplies substantial Recovery on Rounds 1, 2, 5, and 8, with the Rank 6 upgrade strengthening its opening Recovery.
- **Probabilistic disruption:** Arulix can Overwhelm one enemy on Rounds 1, 3, 6, and 8 and Stagger one Physical dealer on Rounds 2, 4, 7, and 9, each at **25%**. Overwhelm can stop Active Commands and Habits on the affected turn; Stagger can stop Basic Attacks but does **not** stop these enemies’ Active Commands.
- **Lane logic:** Vermithor anchors opposite Vhagar and receives its own permanent 12% mitigation. Its Vanguard ability gives Arulix on the Right Flank **8% Damage Received reduction**, useful against matching-lane Moondancer pressure. Malachite occupies the Left Flank opposite Velar.

### Troop rationale

Cavalry has troop advantage over the enemy’s Shieldbearers, increasing damage dealt and reducing damage received formation-wide by an unpublished amount. Malachite and Arulix have Positive Cavalry affinity; Vermithor is neutral rather than negative. Giving up affinity-perfect coverage is justified by Vermithor’s exact Rank 6 Protect mechanic and guaranteed Warrior-priority Physical suppression.

### Enemy threat inventory

- **Velar:** Tactical damage to all three enemies on Rounds 3, 5, 7, and 9; 20% Advantage attempts on even rounds; 24% per-target First-Strike and Slow attempts on Rounds 2, 4, 6, and 8. Its Vanguard is inactive, but Vhagar’s Vanguard gives Left-Flank Velar **16% Tactical Damage Dealt**.
- **Vhagar:** 25% all-enemy Taunt chance each round; 120% Physical damage within adjacency on even rounds; 24% opening Physical/Tactical mitigation at Habit Level 5; 25% non-Basic Physical boost to Right-Flank Moondancer; 35% Advantage/Weakened attempts during Rounds 3–7.
- **Moondancer:** Velar enables Crescent Blade stacks whenever Velar deals Tactical damage or applies Recovery. At Rank 6/Habit Level 5, Moondancer also has 50% Rising Tide attempts on Rounds 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, and 10, doubled when the stated Advantage condition applies. Its even-round Physical Command reaches a **120% rate at Habit Level 5 and doubles at four Rising Tide stacks**. Each stack also reduces Moondancer’s Damage Received by 2%.

### Threat coverage and exclusions

**Direct coverage**

- Cavalry’s troop advantage applies against the entire Shieldbearer formation.
- Vermithor’s opening 15% Physical suppression explicitly applies to one Warrior.
- Malachite’s Tactical mitigation explicitly applies to Velar’s Tactical damage when it activates.
- Vermithor’s Protect explicitly splits damage from **Commands and Habits** against the protected target.

**Partial coverage**

- Vermithor suppresses only one of the two Warriors, and only for five rounds.
- Arulix’s Strength reduction affects the stat-dependent portion of Physical effectiveness; the repository does not establish the final damage formula.
- Malachite’s mitigation targets only its two *other* allies, leaving Malachite itself outside that particular effect.
- Protect covers Command/Habit damage, not Basic Attacks, and selects the eligible adjacent ally with the fewest troops.
- Stagger can stop Basic Attacks but not Vhagar’s or Moondancer’s Active Commands.

**Probabilistic coverage**

- Protect: **50%** on odd rounds.
- Malachite Tactical mitigation: **35% each round**.
- Arulix Overwhelm and Stagger: **25%** on their scheduled rounds.
- Which Warrior receives Vermithor’s priority suppression is unresolved because both Vhagar and Moondancer satisfy the priority.

**Uncovered threats**

- No Cleanse removes Vhagar’s Taunt, Weakened, Velar’s Slow, or other applied control/negative effects.
- Nothing explicitly removes Rising Tide stacks or prevents Crescent Blade from being assigned to Velar.
- Enemy Advantage and First-Strike remain unless Arulix happens to prevent the relevant activation.
- Basic Attacks receive no dragon-specific blanket mitigation beyond Arulix’s probabilistic Stagger, Arulix’s Strength reduction, the Right-Flank Vanguard reduction, and Cavalry’s troop advantage.
- Unknown proc order prevents claiming that Overwhelm, Protect, or mitigation resolves before a scheduled enemy effect.

### Combat horizon

The line is strongest in the opening cycle: Vermithor’s direct suppression covers Rounds 1–5, Malachite front-loads Recovery, and Arulix begins applying damage and control pressure immediately. The matchup becomes less favorable after Round 5 as Vermithor’s suppression expires and Moondancer has had more opportunities to reach four Rising Tide stacks. Malachite’s recurring Recovery and Arulix’s permanent stat reduction remain useful through later cycles, but unresolved combat does not automatically reset “Start of Combat” effects; no reset should be assumed after Stalemate.

### Alternative check

The strongest credible alternative is **Seasmoke / Arulix / Malachite**, with **Arulix in Vanguard** and Cavalry. It is affinity-perfect and Seasmoke makes up to three separate **20% enemy-positive-effect Cleanse attempts each round**, potentially removing Velar/Vhagar Advantage or First-Strike before those effects continue driving tempo and Moondancer’s Advantage-dependent stack chances.

It ranks lower because those removals are random, chance-based, and subject to unknown proc order, while dropping Vermithor loses guaranteed opening Physical suppression and Rank 6 Protect. The alternative becomes preferable if repeated combat evidence shows enemy Advantage/First-Strike reliably accelerates Moondancer past the four-stack threshold before Vermithor’s mitigation matters, or if Vermithor’s target selection repeatedly suppresses the less dangerous Warrior.
